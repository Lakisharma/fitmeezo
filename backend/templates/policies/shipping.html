Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Region: Republic of India

1. Overview & Coverage

Fitmeezo operates a nationwide multi-category e-commerce platform delivering orders across all serviceable postal PIN codes in India. We partner with India's leading courier and express logistics networks (including Blue Dart, Delhivery, and express surface couriers via automated shipment allocation) to ensure safe, tamper-evident, and timely delivery of your purchases.

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

Cart Subtotal Delivery Charge Estimated Transit Timeline
Orders ₹499.00 and Above FREE Delivery 3 – 7 Business Days
Orders Below ₹499.00 Flat ₹49.00 (Standard Courier Fee) 3 – 7 Business Days

* Shipping fees are calculated per order on the checkout screen prior to payment authorization. Free shipping applies automatically when the final merchandise subtotal reaches ₹499 or more.

3. Processing & Dispatch Timeline

Orders are verified, packed in tamper-proof protective packaging, and handed over to logistics partners within 24 to 48 business hours of successful placement (excluding Sundays and national holidays). In multi-vendor orders, items may be dispatched in separate packages to ensure the fastest possible dispatch from the nearest merchant facility.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is supported across eligible PIN codes. When ordering via COD:

  • Payment must be tendered in Indian Rupees (INR cash or via digital scan-and-pay UPI QR provided by the courier executive) at the exact moment of delivery.
  • Open delivery / inspection before payment is not permitted by our logistics partners. If you find any discrepancy or defect after receiving the parcel, our 7-Day Return Policy fully protects your purchase.

5. Shipment Tracking

As soon as your parcel is dispatched from our fulfillment facility or registered seller hub, an SMS and email notification containing the courier name and Airway Bill (AWB) tracking number will be sent to your registered contact details. You can also monitor your delivery status live on our Track Your Order page.

6. Damaged or Tampered Parcels

If you notice that the outer packaging has been severely crushed, torn, or the security seal has been broken upon delivery, please refuse delivery and immediately alert our helpline at {{ SUPPORT_PHONE|default:'+91 940885323' }} or email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL|default:'support@fitmeezo.com' }}.
