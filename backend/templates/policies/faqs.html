How Can We Help You Today?

Explore our frequently asked questions or type a keyword below to find answers on shipping, returns, payment options, and accounts.

You can track your parcel live anytime using our Track Your Order page. Enter your Order Number (e.g. FM-20261010-AB12) and your registered contact email or mobile number. If you have an account, you can also view order status under My Account > My Orders.

Fitmeezo provides FREE standard delivery on all orders where the subtotal is ₹499.00 or higher. For orders under ₹499.00, a flat courier fee of ₹49.00 is applied at checkout. Full details are available in our Shipping Policy.

Orders are dispatched from our partner hubs within 24–48 business hours. Most deliveries across India reach your doorstep within 3 to 7 business days depending on your PIN code and location.

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy starting from the date of confirmed delivery. Eligible items must be unused, unwashed, and returned in their original packaging with all brand tags and accessories intact. Innerwear and personal hygiene items are non-returnable.

Once our fulfillment team receives and inspects the returned item (within 48 hours), refunds are processed immediately. For online prepaid orders, funds reflect in your original payment method in 5–7 business days. For Cash on Delivery orders, funds are deposited to your bank account via NEFT/IMPS within 3–5 business days.

We support a full suite of secure payment options:
  • UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps.
  • Debit & Credit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express.
  • Net Banking: Over 50+ supported Indian banks.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Available on eligible PIN codes.
