Fitmeezo Cookie Policy

Updated: October 2026 • Strict Operational Privacy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small cryptographic text files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone by our web servers when you browse Fitmeezo. They are essential for enabling basic website functionality such as maintaining your active login session, remembering items placed into your shopping cart, and securing form submissions against malicious cyber attacks.

2. The Actual Cookies We Use

Fitmeezo utilizes exclusively strictly necessary first-party cookies required for the reliable operation of our e-commerce platform:

Cookie Identifier Category Operational Purpose Lifespan
sessionid Essential Authentication Maintains your authenticated customer session, cart items, and wishlist selections while browsing across different catalog categories. Session / 2 Weeks
csrftoken Security & CSRF Defense Protects your account against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks when submitting payment, address, and login forms. 1 Year (Standard Django)
messages User Experience Stores temporary flash notification alerts (e.g. order placed, review submitted) that clear after display. Session

3. Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

Fitmeezo does not deploy third-party advertising tracking pixels, cross-site social surveillance cookies, or invasive behavioral retargeting cookies. Our platform focuses entirely on providing you with direct, uncompromised e-commerce shopping without selling your browsing footprints.

4. Controlling Cookies in Your Browser

You can choose to disable or block cookies through your individual browser preferences (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that because our cookies are strictly essential for core functions, disabling cookies will prevent you from logging into your account, maintaining a shopping cart, or completing checkouts.
