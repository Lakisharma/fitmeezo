3. Escalation & Resolution Timelines

Our statutory grievance handling procedure operates under strict legal deadlines:

  • Step 1 (First Response): Before filing a formal grievance, we encourage customers to reach out to our general helpdesk at Contact Us, where standard queries are typically resolved within 24 hours.
  • Step 2 (Formal Acknowledgment): When a formal complaint is escalated to the Grievance Officer, an official acknowledgment and unique ticket number are issued within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Step 3 (Investigation & Resolution): Every grievance is thoroughly investigated in coordination with the fulfilling merchant, logistics carrier, or payment gateway. A written resolution is provided within 30 calendar days (1 month) from the date of complaint registration.

4. Submitting a Formal Complaint

When contacting the Grievance Officer, please include:

  • Your Full Name and registered contact phone number / email address.
  • Order Number (e.g. FM-20261010-AB12) or transaction reference number.
  • Clear, chronological description of the grievance and any prior correspondence with support.
