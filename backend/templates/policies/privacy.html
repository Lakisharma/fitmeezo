Privacy Policy & Customer Data Commitment

Effective Date: October 2026 • Governing Jurisdiction: Republic of India

1. Scope & Commitment

Fitmeezo ("Fitmeezo", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy details the exact data we collect, why we collect it, how it is encrypted and retained, and your rights concerning your personal information when utilizing our website (https://fitmeezo.com).

2. Information We Actually Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide e-commerce shopping services:

  • Account Registration & Authentication: Full name, verified email address, optional contact mobile number, and cryptographic password hash (PBKDF2/SHA-256 algorithm). We never view or store plaintext passwords.
  • Order & Delivery Information: Recipient full name, delivery contact phone number, detailed street address, locality, city, state, and 6-digit postal PIN code required for logistics dispatch.
  • Shopping & Interaction Data: Shopping cart contents, wishlist selections, purchase history, customer reviews submitted, and returns requested.
  • Technical & Session Identifiers: IP address recorded during inquiry/order placement for fraud prevention, browser type, and essential security cookies (Django sessionid and csrftoken).

3. Payment Information & PCI-DSS Compliance

All digital online payments (UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking) are processed directly via Razorpay, an authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment aggregator in India. Fitmeezo does not store, process, or view your debit/credit card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs, or banking passwords. Transaction settlement occurs via secure encrypted tokens.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Fulfilling orders, preparing packing slips, and coordinating doorstep delivery via logistics partners.
  • Transmitting order confirmations, tracking alerts, and invoice PDFs to your registered email.
  • Facilitating customer returns, replacements, and automated refunds.
  • Preventing e-commerce fraud, unauthorized account takeovers, and bot submissions.

5. Zero Sale of Personal Data

Fitmeezo does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with data brokers, advertising syndicates, or unauthorized third parties. We share recipient delivery details exclusively with courier and logistics partners (such as Blue Dart, Delhivery) strictly to complete physical delivery.

6. Customer Rights & Access Controls

You maintain full control over your personal account information:

  • You can view and modify your profile details anytime in My Profile.
  • You can add, edit, or delete saved addresses in Saved Addresses.
  • You may request complete account deactivation and data deletion by contacting our grievance team.

7. Grievance Redressal & Privacy Queries

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our designated Grievance Officer at grievance@fitmeezo.com or via our Grievance Information page.
