Return & Refund Policy

Window: 7 Days from Delivery • Full Refund & Doorstep Reverse Pickup

1. 7-Day Return Window

At Fitmeezo, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. If you receive an item that is defective, damaged in transit, incorrectly dispatched, or fails to meet your expectations, you may initiate a return request within 7 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a full refund or replacement, returned items must comply with the following conditions:

  • Unused & Undamaged: Merchandise must be in its pristine original condition, unworn, unwashed, and without signs of wear, stains, or fragrance.
  • Original Packaging & Brand Tags: Product boxes, manufacturer tags, authenticity cards, warranty booklets, and all accompanying accessories must remain intact.
  • Original Invoice: The customer must retain the order invoice or digital receipt available in the customer dashboard.

3. Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene, safety, and health regulations, the following product categories are strictly non-returnable unless delivered in damaged or defective condition:

  • Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, and personal hygiene products.
  • Cosmetics, perfumes, and skincare items with unsealed or opened packaging.
  • Customized, tailored, or personalized merchandise.

4. Step-by-Step Return Process

  1. Submit Request: Log in to your account, visit My Orders, locate your delivered purchase, and click "Return Items". Select the return reason and upload photos if damaged.
  2. Approval & Pickup: Our support team reviews and approves requests within 24 to 48 hours. A courier agent will be dispatched to collect the parcel from your original delivery address with zero return shipping cost.
  3. Quality Inspection: Once the returned merchandise reaches our fulfillment hub, our quality assurance team inspects the item within 48 hours.
  4. Refund Dispatch: Upon successful inspection, the refund is triggered immediately to your preferred refund method.

5. Refund Timelines & Methods

Original Payment Method Refund Route Processing Timeline
Prepaid (Razorpay / UPI / Cards / Net Banking) Credited back to the original source bank/card/UPI account 5 to 7 Business Days
Cash on Delivery (COD) Direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) via secure customer-provided bank details 3 to 5 Business Days after bank details verification

6. Order Cancellation Prior to Dispatch

Orders can be cancelled at zero charge directly from your account before the merchant has marked them as packed and dispatched. For prepaid orders cancelled prior to shipment, 100% of the payment is refunded instantly back to the source account.
