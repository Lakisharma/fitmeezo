Refund & Cancellation Policy

Hassle-Free Cancellations & Direct Bank / Source Account Refunds

1. Order Cancellation (Prior to Dispatch)

You have the right to cancel your order at any time before it has been marked as shipped by the fulfilling merchant or platform warehouse.

  • Cancellation Process: Simply go to My Orders in your account dashboard, find the order in 'Pending', 'Confirmed', or 'Processing' status, and click "Cancel Order".
  • Cancellation Fee: There are zero cancellation charges or penalty deductions.
  • Prepaid Orders: If payment was completed online via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking), a 100% refund of the transaction total (including any delivery fee) is triggered automatically upon cancellation confirmation.

2. Cancellation After Dispatch (In Transit)

If your order has already been handed over to our logistics partner and is in transit, cancellation cannot be completed via the online dashboard. In this case, you may simply decline/refuse acceptance of the parcel when the courier executive arrives at your doorstep. Once the shipment returns to our warehouse, your refund will be processed.

3. Post-Delivery Returns & Refunds (7 Days)

Once delivered, our 7-Day Return Policy takes effect. You have 7 days from the delivery date to initiate a return request if:

  • The product is defective, damaged, or malfunctioning.
  • The delivered product differs from what was ordered (incorrect size, color, or model).
  • The item is unwashed, unused, and in original packaging with tags intact.
