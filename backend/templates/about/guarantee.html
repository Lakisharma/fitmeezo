Fitmeezo 100% Genuine Guarantee

Zero Counterfeits • Verified Sellers • Complete Buyer Security

1. Our Authenticity Pledge

At Fitmeezo, we believe that when an Indian consumer buys online, they should never have to question the authenticity of what arrives in their parcel. Every product listed across our electronic, fashion, lifestyle, and home categories is guaranteed to be 100% authentic, brand-new, and sourced through legitimate commercial channels.

2. How We Substantiate Authenticity

Strict Merchant KYC

No seller can list products on Fitmeezo anonymously. We mandate valid GSTIN certificates, verified PAN cards, physical warehouse verification, and bank account confirmation before any seller account is activated.

Sourcing Verification

Merchants must maintain valid brand authorization letters, original manufacturer invoices, or distributor agreements demonstrating unbroken chain of custody for all branded goods.

Tamper-Proof Packaging

Shipments are packaged with security seals to prevent en-route substitution or pilferage by third-party handlers.

3. Zero-Tolerance Anti-Counterfeit Policy

Fitmeezo maintains a strict, zero-tolerance standard against unauthorized replicas, grey-market imports, and fake products. In the event that any seller lists counterfeit goods:

  • Their merchant account is suspended immediately and their product catalog removed.
  • Their pending payout balances are frozen for investigation.
  • Details are reported to intellectual property rights owners and relevant legal authorities.

4. Complete Customer Recourse & 100% Refund

In the improbable scenario where you believe an item received is not genuine, our customer protection policy ensures you are completely covered:

  • Notify our support team within the 7-day return window.
  • We will arrange a free doorstep pickup for inspection.
  • If authenticity cannot be substantiated, you will receive an immediate 100% full refund including all delivery fees.
